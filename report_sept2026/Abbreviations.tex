\begin{tabular}{p{3.2cm} p{10.6cm}}
$S_n$, $A_n$     & Symmetric group on $n$ items; alternating (even) subgroup \\
$\Sigma$         & Input alphabet: the set of group elements a token may take \\
$\gen{\Sigma}$   & Group generated by the alphabet (its closure under composition) \\
$\ell(g)$        & Transposition length: fewest transpositions whose product is $g$ \\
$\rho$           & A representation: a homomorphism from a group to invertible matrices \\
$D(g)$           & Order requirement of element $g$ (Definition~\ref{def:demand}) \\
$n_h$            & Number of Householder factors per token (the order) \\
$\beta$          & Strength of a generalized Householder factor, $\beta\in[0,2]$ \\
$\lambda_{t,i}$  & Continue probability emitted by the halting gate for factor $i$ at token $t$ \\
$g_{t,i}$        & Monotone gate applied to factor $i$ at token $t$ \\
$n_t$            & Effective order of token $t$: the number of factors it uses \\
$\gamma$         & Weight of the compute-budget penalty \\
$p$              & Mixture rate: probability that a drawn token is a high-demand 5-cycle \\
$\mathbb{E}[D]$  & Mean order requirement over the data: the cost of an exact allocation \\
\end{tabular}
